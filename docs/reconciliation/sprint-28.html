Implemented launch-control sequence

Exact-build release candidate
A launch program can lock only to a Sprint-27-certified release/build SHA. Changed code cannot reuse launch approval from another build.
Bounded Kenya beta cohort
Enrollment is Kenya-only, invitation-based, consent/evidence-backed and capped. Cohort expansion cannot bypass the configured authorization ceiling.
Privacy-minimized cohort membership
Raw participant identity is hashed before launch persistence. The PostgreSQL lifecycle smoke proves raw subject identifiers are not stored in the cohort record.
Launch evidence package
The exact build gets a hashed manifest spanning release validation, security, clinical, privacy, regulatory, accessibility, performance, DR, incident, rollback and in-scope operations evidence.
Operational monitoring
Pre-beta and active-rollout snapshots consume database/DR/privacy lifecycle readiness, incident state, verified crisis-resource validity, and incident/rollback evidence.
Observed-beta rule
Limited launch cannot follow a paper beta: at least one participant must be enrolled and a GREEN snapshot must be captured strictly after activation and enrollment.
Kenya launch gate
Only a separate Release Authority may authorize beta or limited launch. Required external authority evidence is derived from human regulatory applicability review and remains fail-closed when missing/expired.
Emergency hold and resume
Incident, safety, security or release authorities can pause active rollout immediately. Resumption is Release-Authority-only and re-runs launch readiness.
Post-launch review
Review is bound to the launched build and actual observation window, with monitoring/evidence references and explicit CONTINUE/HOLD/ROLLBACK/EXPAND-REQUIRES-NEW-AUTHORIZATION outcomes.
No CI legal authority
CI test reviewers exercise the state machine and persistence only. They never become real clinical, DPO, AI-safety, security, regulatory or release approvals.

Real launch boundary

Sprint 28 certifies the software controls for a controlled beta and limited Kenya launch. It does not create an actual beta cohort, government registration, clinical approval, Data Protection Officer approval, AI-safety approval, security approval or production Release Authority decision.

A real rollout remains blocked until the exact production build has genuine Sprint-27 governance attestations, an attributable Kenya regulatory applicability determination, all external authority evidence that determination says is required, a current GREEN operational launch snapshot, an approved bounded cohort, and a real Release Authority decision.
