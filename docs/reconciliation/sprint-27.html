Production authorization boundary

This Sprint certifies that the release-assurance mechanism works. It does not fabricate a real clinical, privacy, regulatory, AI-safety, security or legal approval. The PostgreSQL certification smoke deliberately uses TEST-ONLY reviewers to prove persistence and separation-of-duties behavior.

For a real Risk-Class A production build, MindOS remains blocked until the exact build has real attributable clinical, AI-safety, Data Protection Officer, Kenya regulatory and security attestations, followed by a separate Release Authority decision. Where external statutory registration applies, verified external evidence must also exist.
