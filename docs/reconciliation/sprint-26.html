Implemented

Privacy lifecycle orchestration
Erasure/restriction jobs track primary DB, object, vector, search, analytics and cache dependencies; completion is impossible until all required stores acknowledge.
Derived-store evidence
Per-store acknowledgement, blocked and failed receipts prevent false “deletion complete” states.
Structured-log hardening
Sensitive fields and message text are redacted, injection newlines are removed, and log messages are bounded.
Reliability hierarchy
Safety > Data Integrity > Privacy/Security > Identity/Core > Care > AI/Community > Analytics/Experiments.
Graceful degradation
AI, payment, notification, search, analytics, community and primary-DB failures have explicit safe behaviors; safety static paths remain independent.
Explicit recovery targets
RPO/RTO targets are configured per service; the platform does not invent operational defaults.
Tested restore evidence
A backup is not accepted as recovery evidence unless restore, integrity and migration compatibility pass within configured targets.
Persistent migration ledger
Native PostgreSQL migration history is now written to schema_migrations, surviving process restarts and DR restores.
Hardening observability
Database/event health projections exclude raw request bodies, journals, AI prompts, clinical notes, secrets and tokens.
Launch readiness
Database health, DR restore evidence and blocked privacy lifecycle jobs participate in a release-blocking hardening readiness view.

Hardening invariants

  • A privacy lifecycle job cannot report COMPLETE while a required derived store is pending.
  • AI outage cannot make safety resources unavailable.
  • Primary DB uncertainty moves writes to safe mode; success must not be fabricated.
  • Analytics/experiments shed before safety/data-integrity/privacy workloads under backpressure.
  • A backup without a successful tested restore is not valid recovery evidence.
  • Recovery readiness requires explicit configured RPO/RTO values and measured restore evidence.
  • Logs and observability are not exempt from privacy controls.
