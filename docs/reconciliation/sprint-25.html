Implemented

Governed event contracts
Only explicitly approved source events enter the analytics pipeline.
Privacy minimization
Health/clinical payloads contribute to aggregate measurement without copying raw journal, AI, assessment or clinical text into ordinary analytics.
Pseudonymization
Subject identifiers are transformed to salted stable pseudonyms; direct identifiers are excluded from aggregate dimensions.
Versioned metric registry
Metric methodology versions are immutable and historical dashboards retain the applicable version.
Evidence semantics
Descriptive/correlational/comparative/causal evidence levels are explicit; metrics cannot silently upgrade to causal claims.
Cohort suppression
Low-count cohorts are suppressed rather than exposed as mental-health analytics.
Governed dashboards
Safety, Product, Business and Outcome dashboards show approved aggregates or explicit NO_GOVERNED_DATA states.
Experiment policy
Crisis/safety safeguards cannot be withheld by ordinary experiments; pricing cannot target inferred distress/vulnerability.
PostgreSQL persistence
Migration 023 persists metric versions and suppressed/governed aggregates while using the canonical analytics_events raw-minimized layer.
Event bridge
Canonical domain events flow through the minimization transformer before analytics persistence.

Canonical invariants

  • Outcome / Safety / Care Access outrank engagement/activity as success evidence.
  • Raw journal text, private AI conversation text and clinical notes are excluded from ordinary analytics storage.
  • No individual mental-health profiles are exposed through dashboard APIs.
  • Metric changes create new versions; incompatible historical definitions are not silently recomputed.
  • Business metrics with no finance-owned aggregate return NO_GOVERNED_DATA rather than fabricated values.
  • Safety false-positive/false-negative rates are not inferred from ordinary runtime events without governed review sets.
  • Causal language is blocked unless the metric methodology is explicitly governed as causal.
